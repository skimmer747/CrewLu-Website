Mini golf · iPhone and iPad

Putt Off

Tiny courses. Big trash talk.

Pocket mini golf: 18 quick holes, one thumb, real physics, and courses that never repeat.

Free. No ads, no accounts, no tracking.

The daily course

Every day at the flip, a brand-new course appears, and the whole world plays the same 18 holes. You get one round a day. Make it count.

Share a course, start a feud

Any course can travel as a link. Tap a friend's link and that exact course opens on your phone: same holes, same par, honest to the last bumper.

Play together

Up to eight players on one course through Game Center, turn by turn, with sudden-death playoffs when it's tied.

Keep score forever

High score boards, saved courses, player cards, handicaps, streaks and a shelf full of trophies, all synced quietly through iCloud.

The gopher lives under the green and takes every bounce personally. The ducks would like you to play through. The windmill has never once apologized.
